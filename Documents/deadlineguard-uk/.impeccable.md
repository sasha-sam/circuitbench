## Design Context

### Users
UK business founders, directors, and company secretaries managing their Companies House obligations. They are busy, time-poor, and treat compliance as a cost of doing business — not something they enjoy. They want fast, accurate information without friction.

### Brand Personality
Sharp, trustworthy, detailed.
- NOT: bubbly, startup-y, gradient-heavy, or "AI-tool" generic
- YES: editorial precision, high contrast, purposeful — like a quality British financial publication gone digital

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme. Editorial and precise, inspired by Cognition.ai: left-aligned content, very high typographic contrast, clean without being cold. Think FT.com meets a modern developer tool — serious but fast.

Fonts: Barlow Condensed (display headlines, weight 800) + Mulish (body, weight 400–600)
Colors: Near-white background with very subtle tint, near-black text, deep forest green accent (emerald-800 range) — not violet, not blue, not cyan. Green reads as compliance/money/trustworthy in UK context.

### Design Principles
1. **Left-align confidence** — centered layouts feel generic; left-aligned editorial text feels designed and authoritative
2. **Extreme typographic scale** — headlines 3–5× larger than body; big things should be huge, not just bigger
3. **One accent, used sparingly** — deep green touches only where they earn attention (CTAs, risk labels, key numbers)
4. **No decoration tax** — no gradients, no glass, no glow, no side-stripe cards; every visual element must earn its place
5. **Precision over flourish** — tight spacing in dense sections, generous space around hero content; rhythm not uniformity
